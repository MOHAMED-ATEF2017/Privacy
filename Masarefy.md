# Privacy Policy for Masarefy

**Last Updated: March 3, 2026**

## Introduction

Masarefy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you register and use Masarefy, we may collect the following personal information:

- **Account Information**: Email address, username, and profile information when you sign up or sign in with Google
- **Financial Data**: Your spending records, payment information, transaction history, and budget data that you manually enter into the app
- **Authentication Data**: Information provided through Google Sign-In

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: App usage statistics, feature interactions, and preferences
- **Local Storage**: Application settings and preferences stored on your device

### Push Notifications
We collect device tokens to send you:
- Payment reminders
- Budget alerts
- App updates and notifications

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

1. **Account Management**: Creating and managing your user account
2. **Service Delivery**: Providing core expense tracking and budget management features
3. **Data Synchronization**: Syncing your financial data across your devices
4. **Notifications**: Sending relevant alerts and reminders about your spending
5. **App Improvement**: Analyzing usage patterns to improve app functionality
6. **Customer Support**: Responding to your requests and providing technical support

## Data Storage and Security

### Cloud Storage
- Your financial data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore
- All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols
- We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access

### Local Storage
- Some preferences and settings are stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms
- Local data is protected by your device's security features

### Data Retention
- We retain your data for as long as your account is active
- You can request deletion of your data at any time
- Upon account deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days
